# Milestone 6.5 - Risk Trend Chart

## What changed
- Added `RiskTrendChart` using `recharts`.
- Installed the chart dependency and connected the component to `GET /reviews?limit=50`.
- Embedded the chart on the reviews list page above the review cards.
- Added loading, empty, and error states for the chart independently from the review list.

## Why this matters
PatchProof can now show how review risk changes over time. This makes the dashboard useful as a project-health view, not just a table of past runs.

## Critical implementation details
- The chart uses saved review rows from the backend, not mock data.
- The x-axis is the review date and the y-axis is the persisted risk score.
- The tooltip shows repository name, risk score, risk level, and merge recommendation for the hovered point.
